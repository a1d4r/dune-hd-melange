# Changelog

**English version**

## 0.70.0 — 26.11.2026
- **Two Melange items in the Dune card.** The **Play...** window of a movie or series card in the Dune Movies catalog now has two items. "Melange" opens the Melange card on top of the Dune card, on the season and episode open there, and "Melange — watch" works as the old "Melange" item did. If you reordered this window before ("Configure List..."), the new item is at the end.
- **PLAY in the Dune card.** When **PLAY** starts an item without the window (for example, on a movie from "My Collection"), it does what "Melange — watch" does. Continuing a started movie works as before.
- **The Melange card always.** The "Own card" setting is gone: **ENTER** on a poster in the catalog opens the Melange card on any firmware. If you had turned it off, you'll see the Melange card after the update. To open the Dune card, press **MENU** → "Dune card" in the Melange card.
- **Balancers "for the Lampa app only".** Lampac-compatible servers mark some balancers as working only in the Lampa app. Melange used to hide them without asking. Now it asks them as well and hides only the ones that fail, which still show up in "Balancer errors". A title may get more balancers than before.
- **Quality from the balancer name.** If a server writes the quality in the balancer name in brackets ("vkmovie 「4K」"), Melange shows it as a quality badge and removes the brackets from the name.
- **Kinopoisk ID from the catalog.** If you open a movie from the Dune card and the Lampac server gives no Kinopoisk ID, Melange takes it from its catalog, so balancers that search by Kinopoisk find the movie.
- Fixed: in a browser on the Dune itself (such as Yandex Browser), "Save to a file" and "Download the log" on the settings page failed to download. Now they download there too.

## 0.69.1 — 25.11.2026
- **POP UP in the TV tab, as in the catalog.** In the TV tab of the home screen, **POP UP** now has the same items as in the catalog (Movies, Series, Continue Watching, Favorites, On TorrServer, Search, Settings) and also Open Melange. Each opens its Melange screen, and **RETURN** brings you back to TV. On a row name the menu also has **Configure List...**, and rows you turn on there appear in TV usually a few seconds later.
- **"On TorrServer" as a list.** The section now looks like the "list" view of the Dune Movies section. A line has only the TorrServer icon and the torrent name. The poster of the selected torrent is on the right of the screen, with the number of files, the size, the date added and the full torrent name under it. All firmware versions show the same list; before, it was poster rows, or a grid on older firmware. To remove a torrent from TorrServer, press **POP UP** on it, choose "Remove from TorrServer" and confirm.
- **Wallpaper behind "On TorrServer".** Melange puts the Dune wallpaper behind the list, the same one as above the icon bar of the catalog. In 0.69.0 the background above the rows was black.
- Fixed: when you watched a movie or an episode from the Melange card through the release list (Debrid, TorrServer, Lampac), the card kept showing the progress from when you opened it. After you return from the player, the card now shows the new progress: check marks on watched episodes, bars on the frames, Continue based on what you just watched, and the line above the buttons.
- Fixed: an episode that started by itself when the previous one ended was not saved in the Dune history. Now the Dune keeps where you stopped it and its watched mark, and Continue takes you to it (Debrid, Lampac and TorrServer, on r25 firmware). If you play an episode you started earlier and didn't finish, the episode after it is still not saved when it starts by itself after that one ends. A next episode you had started before and didn't finish now plays from the beginning when it comes on this way or with **P+**, and the Dune doesn't show the "Resume playback" window for it. But if you play an episode you started earlier and didn't finish, the window still shows up for the next one.

## 0.69.0 — 23.11.2026
- **"On TorrServer" section.** It shows all the torrents already on TorrServer, newest first, movies and series on one screen. They may have been added from a phone with NUM, KinoTrend or Lampa, or by Melange with "Add & Play". Open it with the icon in the top bar of the catalog (a grey circle with a lightning bolt, after the heart) or with "On TorrServer" in **POP UP**. A tile has the poster and title from TorrServer, and above the rows you see the name, the number of files, the size and the date it was added. Melange doesn't change or delete torrents on TorrServer. If TorrServer doesn't answer or torrents are turned off in the Melange settings, the section says what happened and shows a "Settings" button. More in [Torrents and TorrServer](docs/torrserver.en.md).
- **Playing from "On TorrServer".** **ENTER** on a torrent with one video starts the player right away. A pack or a series opens a file list: seasons are grouped, watched files have a check mark, and the cursor is on the next unwatched one. The next episodes play in order (**P+**/**P−**). The player offers to resume where you stopped, and what you started shows up in "Recent" on the Dune home screen and continues from there.

## 0.68.0 — 23.11.2026
- **Files from a USB drive and the internal disk.** Movies and series from the Dune media library on a USB drive or the disk inside the Dune now play from the Melange card too, and their posters get the disk icon. Before, only network folders worked.
- **Disk icon on posters.** Movies and series from the Dune media library have a disk icon in the top right corner of the poster: in the catalog rows, Show all, search, Favorites, Continue Watching, the rows of the Melange card and the filmography on an actor's page. A series gets it if at least one episode is on the disk. Melange doesn't check whether the NAS is on, so the icon shows even when it's off. Melange goes through a library of thousands of movies in the background in about half a minute after the catalog first opens, and the icons appear the next time you open it. The remote doesn't lag meanwhile.
- Fixed: movies the Dune media library marked as bonus material had no "From disk" line. Now they play from the disk too.
- Fixed: Watch on a movie stored in the Dune media library as a Blu-ray folder (BDMV) said "The movie file is not on the disk". Now the Dune plays the folder itself: it may ask how to play the disc, then opens it in its Blu-ray player with chapters. If you stop the movie before the end, it shows up in Continue Watching.

## 0.67.0 — 22.11.2026
- **Releases with a different year or title.** Movies such as "Kill Bill: The Whole Bloody Affair" are no longer empty. Releases whose year or title on the tracker differs from the movie card are gathered in the "Unconfirmed" group at the top of the list (on the Melange server), and the "Check" line in the release card says why.
- **Movies and series from your disk.** If a movie or an episode is in the Dune media library (network folders on a NAS or a computer), the Melange card plays it straight from the disk. A movie shows a "From disk" line with the quality and file size, episodes have a disk icon on the frame, and a season says "N on disk". The next episodes play from the disk one after another, also from the next season's folder. If the next episode is not on the disk, Melange offers "Choose a release" or "Stop". Melange waits up to 25 seconds for a sleeping NAS, and if it still does not answer, there is "Retry". With several copies, Melange asks "Which copy to watch?", and you can switch copies with MENU → "Copy on disk…"; releases from the internet are in MENU → "Watch from…". More in [Movies and series from your disk](docs/library.en.md).
- **Progress in the movie card.** A started movie shows a bar and "0:58 left" in its Melange card, as in the panel above the catalog rows, and a watched one shows a full bar and a check mark. The card used to say "Watched 40% · 0:58 left".
- **Episodes stopped in the credits count as watched.** An episode you stop or skip with "Next episode" during the credits is now marked as watched in Melange and on the Dune season screen, and "Continue" goes to the next one.
- Fixed: in the "Episode is not in this release" window, "Choose a release" opened the list, and RETURN from it went back to a stuck player. Now the player stops first. The same goes for the error shown when the release list does not open.
- Fixed: new Russian series and movies that have no IMDb ID in TMDB could show no releases for a few hours after they were first opened. Started from the Melange card, they now have releases right away. From the Dune card, they work as before.

## 0.66.0 — 20.11.2026
- **New look for the release list.** The release list is redrawn with simpler tags, and a row shows only the main ones, such as Vision and Atmos.
- **BDRip.** A BDRip or BRRip release with BluRay quality gets its own BDRip tag, and the "Video" field of the card and of "Details" says BDRip instead of BluRay.
- **Language flags.** The voice-over languages of a row are flags. A flag means the language is confirmed: by the file's tracks, a RUS(int)/RUS(ext) mark in the name, a dubbing studio, or the words "Дубляж", "ДБ", "MVO", "ПМ". A language that is only a guess (Cyrillic in the release name, addon flags with nothing in the name to back them) adds one "?" after the flags. Flags that do not fit (more than two, or not enough room) fold into "+N", and then the row has no "?". If there are no languages but the release is marked MULTI, DUAL, DUB or ORIG, one word shows on a small plate, like the "?": the first of these that applies. In the card, a guessed language has a question mark: "Languages: JA, RU?".
- **Russian subtitles tag.** A tag with a subtitles icon and "RU" shows when the release has Russian subtitles according to the indexer, a file probe or the Crunchyroll list. The English interface shows RU too.
- **Russian in Voices.** For a release marked RUS(int)/RUS(ext), "Voices" now says "Russian". Russian used to be missing there.
- **Same episode, another release / Same movie, another release.** If a release fails to start and the list has the same file in another release, the error window has a button that starts it.
- **No more "already in the Debrid library" sorting.** The Melange AIOStreams template no longer sets it, so a release that a failed probe added to the service does not rise to the top of later lists. Melange also removes this key from configs it created before.
- Fixed: when an addon queries other addons, the list and "Details" showed trackers as "Comet/StremThru/Rutracker". Now only the tracker, the last link, shows, and repeats are dropped regardless of case.
- Fixed: when Comet did not return the video, the error window said "HTTP 200, N KB" instead of the reason.

The player opens once 32 MB are in, and the picture shows up almost at once instead of a long black screen. A slow release shows before the player, and you can press "Cancel". - **Movies without an IMDb ID play through Lampac.** If Lampac is among your sources, the Melange card of such a movie or series has **Watch**, with "Lampac only: no IMDb ID" next to the buttons. It has no releases: Watch, the Dune card and Continue Watching go straight to the Lampac search, and "Watch from…" and "Get films from…" list only Lampac sources. Lampac searches by title and year, so the list may include other movies with the same title. Without Lampac the card says "No IMDb ID: no releases".

Balancers that used to fail, such as ZetflixDB, now work. Balancers get the title Lampac knows the movie by and find it more accurately, Continue Watching included.

The row's tiles go first; on r25 the row updates in place, on r24 the next time you open it. On the Continue Watching and Favorites screens (from a row, the clock, the heart, Fav 1 or Fav 2), on r25 firmware the missing details load while the screen is open and the tiles update in place. On r24 firmware the screen waits up to about 2 seconds as it opens to fill in the visible tiles, and the rest comes the next time you open it.
- **Backup** on the settings page now saves Favorites too, and loading the backup brings them back.

## 0.58.0 — 13.11.2026
- **Source profiles: choose where films come from right in the release list.** For those who use both Debrid and torrents, or more than one AIOStreams server. Turn it on on the settings page: **Advanced** → **Source profiles** (off by default). Then the "Where to get films" screen becomes the profile editor. A profile is a set of source settings under its own name: Debrid, "Torrents" or your own config link, the AIOStreams server (one of the list or your own, for example on a NAS), the template and JacRed. Debrid and TMDB keys, TorrServer, download servers and the other settings are shared by all profiles.
