Quality badges `nb2_*.png` are made from the white variants of [github.com/kingsizew/badges](https://github.com/kingsizew/badges) (commit `57c624e`), resized for the Dune screen.

`menu_search.png`, the magnifier of the "Search" item in the catalog MENU, is drawn in the style of the firmware `small_icons` (white, 48×48), which have no magnifier.

`cat2_*.png` of the info panel of the catalog: `cat2_mask.png` is the firmware's background mask (`shell_ext/icons/mask.png`), darker to the right so the text stays on the dark; `cat2_mask_full.png` is the same mask placed on the whole screen, black around it, for screens whose background covers the whole screen; `cat2_kp.png`, `cat2_imdb.png`, `cat2_age.png` are empty outlined plates, the text over them is drawn by the shell.
