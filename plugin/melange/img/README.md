Quality badges `nb2_*.png` are made from the white variants of [github.com/kingsizew/badges](https://github.com/kingsizew/badges) (commit `57c624e`), resized for the Dune screen.

`cat2_*` of the info panel of the catalog: `cat2_mask.png` is the firmware's background mask (`shell_ext/icons/mask.png`), darker to the right so the text stays on the dark; `cat2_card0.jpg` is our own dark background of the whole screen, shown when a title has no background of its own or while it loads; `cat2_kp.png`, `cat2_imdb.png`, `cat2_age.png` are empty outlined plates, the text over them is drawn by the shell.

`rate1_plate.png` is the plate under the IMDb rating in the corner of a poster in the catalog rows: 52×32, 70 % black, rounded corners; the number over it is drawn by the shell.

`tv_sub_movie.png`, `tv_sub_series.png` are the icons of the sub-items "Movies" and "Series" under the tab "TV" of the main screen (`rows_menu_config.json`, `tv_tab.php`): 100×100, placeholders for now.
