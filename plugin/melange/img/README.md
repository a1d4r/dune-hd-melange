Quality badges `nb2_*.png` of the resolutions (the online list) are made from the white variants of [github.com/kingsizew/badges](https://github.com/kingsizew/badges) (commit `57c624e`), resized for the Dune screen.

`cat2_*` of the info panel of the catalog: `cat2_mask.png` is the firmware's background mask (`shell_ext/icons/mask.png`), darker to the right so the text stays on the dark; `cat2_card0.jpg` is our own dark background of the whole screen, shown when a title has no background of its own or while it loads; `cat2_kp.png`, `cat2_imdb.png`, `cat2_age.png` are empty outlined plates, the text over them is drawn by the shell.

`rate1_plate.png` is the plate under the IMDb rating in the corner of a poster in the catalog rows: 52×32, 70 % black, rounded corners; the number over it is drawn by the shell. `rate1_disk.png` is the badge of a title on the disk in the opposite corner: the same plate 32×32 with a white disk.

`tv_sub_movie.png`, `tv_sub_series.png` are the icons of the sub-items of "TV", "Movies" and "Series", on the main screen (`rows_menu_config.json`, `tv_tab.php`): 100×100, a light outline on transparent.

`card1_*` of the own card of a movie: `card1_pill_l.png`, `card1_pill_r.png` (and `_on_` of the focused button) are the rounded ends of a button, 12×64, the plugin puts a rect of the same colour between them; `card1_play_icon.png`, `card1_play_on_icon.png` are the triangle of "Watch"; `card1_plus_icon.png`, `card1_tick_icon.png` (and `_on_` of the focused button) are the "+" and the tick of the button of "My List" (the font of the firmware has no tick); `card1_tab_l.png`, `card1_tab_r.png` (and `_on_` of the focused one) are the rounded ends of a tab of a season of a series, 26×52, white 16 % for the season shown. `card1_rate.png` is the plate under the IMDb rating on a poster of the rows of the own card and of the page of a person: `rate1_plate.png` scaled to the poster 240×360, 66×40, 70 % black, rounded corners; the number over it is drawn by the shell. `card1_disk_mark.png` is the badge of a title on the disk on those posters, 40×40.

`k_*` are the pictures of the UI kit. Each file name ends with a hash of its pixels, so a changed picture gets a new URL (the shell keeps a picture by its URL even after a reinstall). The plugin takes the URL from the table `mel_k_pic()` in `kit_tokens.php`. `k_corner_*` are the corners of rounded plates and rings, one set per colour: the plugin puts four of them at the corners and fills the edges with rects of the same colour. `k_icon_*` are white [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons (`lucide-static` 0.460.0, ISC), 96×96, and the plugin sets their size and colour. `k_glyph_*` are the glyphs of the video and sound badges on a transparent ground, 40 px high, made from ready packs of [nuviobadges.com](https://nuviobadges.com) (Minimalistic and Minimalist White, with the gaps filled from kingsizew/badges and NardBadges Slim); the plugin puts each on a plate as wide as its column of the list. `k_corner_r5h40_badge_el` and `k_corner_r5h40_badge_er` are the left and right ends of that plate, 5×40 (both corners of a side and the edge between them), with a rect of the same colour between them. `k_badge_subs-ru` (`k_badge_subs_ru.*.png`) is the only `k_badge_*`: the Russian subtitles badge, one picture with its plate. `k_key_info` is the "i" of the INFO key.

`tsl1_logo.png` is the icon of the section "На TorrServer": on the strip of the catalog, in its item of POP UP and on the plate of a tile with no poster. 64×64 with the glyph 48×48 at 8, 8 as the icons of the strip of the shell, grey `#BBBBBB` as the heart, full alpha (the strip sets its own); in the style of the TorrServer logo (a ring, a bolt, "T" and "S" moved off the bolt).

`tsr1_*` of the row "На TorrServer" of the catalog: `tsr1_card.jpg` (1920×1080, our mask in it) and `tsr1_bg.jpg` (1280×720, under `cat2_mask.png`) are the background of the info panel of a torrent not known by the history, with "Логотип фильма вместо названия" on and off: a dark teal ground with the TorrServer logo; `tsr1_line.png` (32×32) is the icon of the line "На TorrServer · …" of the panel of a known one, after the official TorrServer logo.
